Editorial standards

How we curate.

VYRDICT is the gifting encyclopedia: extraordinary objects, thoughtful gestures and unexpected discoveries. Our gift recommendations are curated editorial selections, not numerical scores.

What makes a gift worth featuring?

We look for distinctive design, thoughtfulness, relevance to the recipient or occasion, and an interesting story or experience. Popularity alone does not earn a place in a collection, and placement is not a guarantee of quality or suitability.

Research and shopping information

We aim to identify the exact product and variant, check retailer destinations and distinguish confirmed information from illustrative images, comparable listings or details still being verified. We use brand information, retailer listings and other relevant sources. Editorial research is not hands-on testing unless stated.

Prices, stock, delivery, duties and availability differ by market and may change. Confirm details with the retailer before purchasing.

Editorial independence and commercial relationships

Commercial relationships do not buy editorial placement or recommendations. Some links may earn us a commission at no extra cost to the shopper. We clearly disclose material connections (including affiliate commissions, sponsorships, gifted products and other benefits) close to the relevant recommendation or shopping link. Sponsored placements are labelled separately; a general disclosure or a brand tag alone is not a substitute for a clear disclosure. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Beauty, wellness and safety

Gift selections are general editorial information, not individualized medical advice or a guarantee of safety or effectiveness. Check age suitability, ingredients, allergies, warnings and manufacturer instructions where relevant.

Corrections and suggestions

We welcome interesting gift finds and corrections via Suggest a Product or hello@vyrdict.com. Suggestions are reviewed privately and do not guarantee publication. We may revise or remove a selection when its information changes or an error is identified.

Our research archive

Historical product research may retain its original methodology and ratings. These are separate from the current gifting collections, which do not use numerical ratings.
