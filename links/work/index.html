McCafé.

The coffee run got its own It bag.

  • An everyday coffee carrier became a fashion accessory.
  • The opportunity: make a useful product part of a daily ritual.

What we deliver

  • Trend research: Find habits and cultural moments that fit your product.
  • Launch concept: Develop the idea and key messages.
  • Creator shortlist: Choose creators with the right audience and style.
  • Creator briefs: Explain what to film, show and say.

THE CASE FILE 03BEAUTY / PRODUCT STORYTELLING
BRAND STUDY / NOT CLIENT WORK

rhode.

When the product is the post.

  • An acne patch became part of visible beauty routines and personal style.
  • The opportunity: make the product easy to picture in everyday life.

What we deliver

  • Product positioning: Explain its use and why people should care.
  • Campaign direction: Define the launch look, message and story.
  • Creator briefs: Show how to demonstrate the product in real life.
  • Shot list: Plan close-ups, routine moments and styling shots.
  • Video plan: Develop formats, opening hooks and captions.
  • Content testing: Compare demos with styling posts to learn what works.
