THE VYRDICT APPROACH

How we score opportunities.

VYRDICT’s product ratings help people decide whether viral products are worth it. The framework below assesses opportunities for VYRDICT Studio campaigns.

We consider five dimensions, each worth up to 20 points. Together they form an illustrative opportunity score out of 100, helping us decide when to act, adapt, keep watching—or pass.

These are VYRDICT's editorial assessment categories—not a standardized market metric. Scores depend on the available evidence and are not predictions of virality, sales or campaign performance. Brand collaborations do not influence VYRDICT’s product ratings or guarantee a product review.

The point isn't chasing everything viral. It's knowing which opportunities deserve your resources—and what to do with them.
