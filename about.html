The verdict on what’s trending.

VYRDICT is a product intelligence and discovery platform built to answer a simple question: when a product is everywhere, is it actually worth buying?

VYRDICT is pronounced “verdict”
01

Track the hype.

VYRDICT watches social momentum, search demand, creator spread, launches, restocks and other signals that show when a product is breaking out.

02

Score the evidence.

Viral attention is separated from product value. VYRDICT evaluates consumer evidence, value, momentum and hype risk instead of treating popularity as proof.

03

Make the call.

Every verified product gets a Viral Score, Worth Score and clear editorial verdict so shoppers can move from trend to decision without a review rabbit hole.

How the platform works

Viral is a signal. Worth is a decision.

VYRDICT is designed as an evidence layer between social discovery and purchase. The platform does not assume a product is good because it is popular. It measures attention and product value separately, then shows the evidence behind the score.
