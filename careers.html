Freelance Social Content Creator

RemoteFreelancePart-timeSocial + short-form video

VYRDICT is looking for a creator who genuinely understands TikTok, Reels, internet culture and viral-product conversations. You’ll help turn VYRDICT’s scores, verdicts and product discoveries into content that feels current, useful and native to social — not overly corporate.

What you’ll create

  • 3 short-form videos for TikTok / Reels each week
  • 2 Instagram posts each week, including simple static or carousel concepts
  • Social-first ideas built around viral products, Worth Scores and clear verdicts
  • Timely concepts that respond to trends without losing the VYRDICT point of view

What we’re looking for

  • A strong feel for TikTok, Reels and internet culture
  • Comfort with tools such as Canva, CapCut or similar creator tools
  • Good visual taste, concise writing and an instinct for what makes content feel current
  • Reliable communication and the ability to work independently
  • Interest in beauty, tech, wellness, fashion, fitness, lifestyle or viral consumer products

Interested?

Email us with a link to your portfolio or examples of your work, 2–3 content ideas you’d create for VYRDICT, and your availability.

Students and emerging creators are welcome. We care more about taste, ideas and execution than years of experience.
