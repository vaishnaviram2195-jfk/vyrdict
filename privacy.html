Privacy

Useful data. Minimal baggage.

VYRDICT is designed to collect only the information needed to run the product-discovery experience and research workflow.

Product suggestions

When you use Suggest a Product, VYRDICT may receive the product name, brand, a product or post URL, the platform where you are seeing it, and the optional note you submit. The public suggestion form does not ask for your name or email address.

Suggestions are used as private research input. Submitting a suggestion does not create a public profile, review, or comment.

Site preferences

VYRDICT may store small preference values in your browser, such as your selected Canada or United States shopping region. These values help the site show more relevant retailer options and are not public.

Technical information

Like most websites, the hosting and infrastructure providers used by VYRDICT may process standard technical information needed to serve and secure the site, such as request timestamps, browser/network information, and diagnostic logs.

Shopping links

When you follow a retailer or other third-party link, you leave VYRDICT. The destination site may collect information under its own privacy practices, which VYRDICT does not control.

Analytics

VYRDICT may use limited, privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate behavior such as page views, product opens, searches, and retailer clicks. We aim to avoid collecting information that is unnecessary for understanding how the product is used.

Sale of personal information

VYRDICT does not sell information submitted through the product-suggestion form to advertisers.
