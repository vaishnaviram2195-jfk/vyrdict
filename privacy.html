Privacy

Useful data. Minimal baggage.

VYRDICT is designed to collect only the information needed to run product discovery, optional accounts, saved-product syncing, and the research workflow.

Optional accounts and saved products

You can browse and save products without creating an account. If you choose to sign in, VYRDICT receives the email address used for authentication and an account identifier. Your cloud shortlist stores the identifiers of products you save so those saves can follow you across devices.

Your account email and saved-product list are private account data. VYRDICT does not create a public profile from them.

Passwordless sign-in and email delivery

VYRDICT uses Supabase for authentication and cloud account infrastructure. Passwordless sign-in emails are delivered through Resend using VYRDICT’s authenticated email domain. Your email address is used to provide requested account access and essential account-related messages; VYRDICT does not treat signing in as consent to receive marketing email.

Browser storage and preferences

VYRDICT may store small values in your browser, including saved-product identifiers, the account associated with a local save cache, session information managed by the authentication service, and your selected Canada or United States shopping region. These values support the site experience and are not public.

Product suggestions

When you use Suggest a Product, VYRDICT may receive the product name, brand, a product or post URL, the platform where you are seeing it, and the optional note you submit. The public suggestion form does not ask for your name or email address.

Suggestions are used as private research input. Submitting a suggestion does not create a public profile, review, or comment.

Analytics

VYRDICT uses limited first-party analytics to understand aggregate behavior such as page views, product opens, retailer clicks, saves, and the account sign-in funnel. The analytics system is designed not to store account email addresses, names, persistent user identifiers, or raw search terms.

Technical information and service providers

Hosting, authentication, email-delivery, domain, and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information needed to serve, secure, and diagnose the service, such as request timestamps, browser or network information, authentication events, and diagnostic logs. Providers process this information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Shopping links

When you follow a retailer or other third-party link, you leave VYRDICT. The destination site may collect information under its own privacy practices, which VYRDICT does not control.

Retention and deleting your account

Account information and cloud-synced saves are kept while your VYRDICT account is active and needed to provide account features. A signed-in user can use Delete account on the VYRDICT Account page to permanently remove the VYRDICT authentication account and its cloud-synced saved-product records.

Deleting an account also clears the VYRDICT save cache on the browser where deletion is requested. Local browser data on another device or browser cannot be remotely erased by VYRDICT and may remain until that browser’s site data is cleared. Infrastructure or security logs may also remain for a limited period according to provider retention practices or legal requirements.

Sale and use of personal information

VYRDICT does not sell account email addresses, cloud-synced saved-product lists, or product-suggestion submissions to advertisers. Personal information is used for the purposes described here unless another use is disclosed and permitted.

Questions and privacy requests

For questions about VYRDICT privacy practices or requests relating to your account information, contact hello@vyrdict.com.
