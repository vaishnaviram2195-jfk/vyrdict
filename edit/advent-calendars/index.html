SUKOSHI

2026 Advent Calendar

Thirty-plus full-size products across 24 days.
Dr. Ceuracle Kombucha Essence (150ml), ROUND LAB Birch Juice Cream (80ml) and AROMATICA Rosemary Scalp Scrub (165g) are substantial routine staples.

The VYRDICT take
Buy for skincare restocking. Pass for a makeup-first countdown: several cleansers and lip products make the assortment less varied than the 30+ headline suggests.

Lush

Advent Calendar

A fragrance-led countdown, with a hamper worth keeping.
25 gifts, including 10 gift exclusives. Perfume, body spray and a candle sit alongside bath and body products in a reusable green wicker hamper. North American contents.

The VYRDICT take
Buy for the bath-and-fragrance fan. Ten gifts are bath bombs or bubble bars, so this is a poor fit for a shower-only routine.

DAVIDsTEA

24 Days of Tea

A different little tea ritual behind every door.
24 loose-leaf blends, with two 250ml servings per day. The gingerbread-house calendar mixes caffeinated and caffeine-free options. An infuser is needed.

The VYRDICT take
Buy for someone who enjoys playful, dessert-inspired blends. Pass for a purist wanting only unflavoured tea, or an entirely caffeine-free countdown.

Williams Sonoma

Popcorn Advent Calendar

The countdown that turns into movie night.
24 doors of non-GMO popping corn, sweet-and-savoury seasonings and recipes, made by Indiana’s family-owned Wabash Valley Farms.

The VYRDICT take
Buy for the make-it-together household. The fun is popping and tasting; pass if you want an instant snack. These are raw kernels, and some doors hold seasoning. A popper is not included.

LEGO

Disney Princess Advent Calendar 2026

Little builds, then a whole new way to play.
24 doors, 290 pieces and four microdolls: Ariel, Cinderella, Moana and Mulan. Four animal friends join them, and the box folds into a board game for up to eight players. Ages 5+. Set 43298.

The VYRDICT take
Buy for younger Disney fans and daily play. Pass if you want full-size character figures or a substantial display build: these are microdolls and miniature models.
