# VYRDICT — The Gifting Encyclopedia
Approved direction, November 2026

## Brand and positioning
VYRDICT. — THE GIFTING ENCYCLOPEDIA
Primary tagline: Never give ordinary.
Editorial headline: For the impossible to shop for.
Extraordinary objects. Thoughtful gestures. Unexpected discoveries.
Good taste is a gift. Discover · Save · Gift.
Ivory, espresso, muted burgundy, subtle pink; editorial serif typography, genuinely crisp original product photography, inclusive rather than overly feminine. A digital gifting department store without inventory: visual discovery, independent editorial taste, practical shopping.
Three visitors: explorer (something never seen), purposeful shopper (housewarming under $100), indecisive shopper (what to get him). No required account or unnecessary steps to retailer.

## Homepage and index
Magazine front page, editorial hero, four pathways (I know who I'm shopping for; There's an occasion coming up; I'm just here to discover; I need a little inspiration), Freshly discovered / latest drop. Permanent gift boards and central deduplicated product records, many-to-many tags, never separate boards for each carousel.
The Gift Index: 01 The People (her, him, little ones, couples, parents, pet lovers, colleagues); 02 The Occasions (birthdays, housewarming, weddings/newlyweds, anniversaries, baby showers, graduation, thank you, just because); 03 The Personalities (coffee obsessive, art collector, fashion girl, homebody, eternal traveller, perfectionist, foodie, tech enthusiast); 04 The Particulars (zodiac, personalized, vintage, little luxuries, limited editions, budget).
Signature collections: Gifts She’ll Gatekeep (all parts + perfume in one permanent collection), He Has Everything. Almost., Little People, Big Taste, Happily Ever After, A House With Taste, Never Arrive Empty-Handed, Holiday Collectibles (including Advent), Black Friday Edit as seasonal archive.
Emotional shopping: Make them say WOW; Make it personal; Make them feel spoiled; Make them laugh.
The Zodiac Room / Written in the stars: 12 signs, 12 creative editorial gift lists, unique aesthetics over time; inspiration, not personality predictions.
Navigation: Discover, Gift Index, Gift Finder, Saved. Retain research archive and existing direct URLs.

## Product experience
Original brand/retailer photos, exact product/variant identity, gift note explaining recipient and why it's unusual, shopping links and country availability, optional personalization, currency-specific verified dated price, budget filtering; no invented prices or stock. Distinguish exact, comparable and unresolved items, exclude unresolved from primary shopping catalogue. Preserve original research, Viral/Worth scores on legacy research only unless evidence-backed, editorial ratings optional and clearly labelled. Local saved gifts initially, later accounts and shareable boards. Gift Finder asks recipient, occasion, budget, vibe and returns useful catalogue matches with explicit relaxation/empty states. Never fabricate catalogue entries.

## Discovery and future sections
Weekly Find / Freshly discovered; Secret Stash (independent designers); Gift Passport (Japan, Korea, Italy, India, France, and beyond); Little Luxuries Club; Wishlist; Last-Minute Edit (delivery verified); Collector's Corner; Gift SOS / community submits hard gifting situations; Gift Calendar reminders; creator-curated boards and designer partnerships.
Research original designers, museum shops, international concept stores, design exhibitions, independent makers, reverse image search; prefer novel, shoppable and exceptional over generic volume. Verify identity, photo, merchant, stock and geography, price and affiliate eligibility. Prioritize 100 exceptional gifts over 5,000 ordinary ones.

## Revenue and distribution
VYRDICT owns website, catalogue and audience. Instagram carousel -> relevant VYRDICT board -> approved affiliate tracking link -> merchant checkout -> eligible commission. No inventory, payments or returns. Apply for ShopMy, Skimlinks, Awin; evaluate Impact, Sovrn, Amazon Associates and direct designer partnerships. Use only approved links, one tracking route per destination, clear disclosure near shopping links and in social posts; no competing auto-link scripts. Approval/commission coverage must be confirmed, not assumed. Track outbound clicks, attributed sales, actual payouts, shares, saves, followers per 1,000 views and return visits. Instagram comment-to-DM board links only after messaging permissions approved. Sponsorship transparently disclosed, editorial independence.
Social voice: Gifts for people who are painfully particular; Gifts so good you'll buy two; Never show up empty-handed; Looks expensive, costs less; Things I can't believe actually exist; for him, her, kids, weddings, housewarming, zodiac, global discovery. Weekly Gift Drop newsletter once ready; preserve subscriber permissions and existing delivery until editorial transition is reviewed.
